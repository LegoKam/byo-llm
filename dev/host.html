Walking the Larapinta Trail

The Larapinta Trail is a 223 kilometre walk through the West MacDonnell Ranges in central Australia. It runs from Alice Springs to Mount Sonder and is split into twelve sections, each of which takes a day or two to complete.

Most walkers go between May and August, when the days are mild and teh nights are cold. You will need to carry water between tanks, and you should recieve your food drops before you set out.

What to pack

  • A sleeping bag rated to minus five degrees
  • Three litres of water capacity per person
  • Sun protection: hat, long sleeves and sunscreen

Guided walks are available for the best-known sections if you would rather not carry a full pack.
